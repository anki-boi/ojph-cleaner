- Sticky at the top of the results column (`position: sticky`, inside the site's own column, so it scrolls
  with the board and never overlaps a card). The progress row exists only while a run is going.

  `#ojc-gear`, the view buttons) so `verify-live.mjs` keeps its handles; its label checks read `LABELS`.
